Jenkins with Maven is deploying. Allow 2-3 minutes for the pod to reach Ready state.

Get the admin password:
  kubectl exec -it $(kubectl get pods -l app.kubernetes.io/name=jenkins -o name | head -1) \
    -- cat /bitnami/jenkins/home/secrets/initialAdminPassword

Get the NodePort:
  kubectl get svc -l app.kubernetes.io/name=jenkins -o jsonpath='{.items[0].spec.ports[0].nodePort}'

Open: http://<node-ip>:<nodeport>
