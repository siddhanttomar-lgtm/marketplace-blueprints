Jenkins + Maven is deploying. Allow up to 5 minutes for the JVM and plugins to initialize.

Get the NodePort:
  kubectl get svc {{ .Release.Name }}-jenkins -o jsonpath='{.spec.ports[0].nodePort}'

Retrieve the admin password:
  kubectl get secret {{ .Release.Name }}-jenkins -o jsonpath='{.data.jenkins-password}' | base64 -d

Open http://<node-ip>:<nodeport> in your browser.
