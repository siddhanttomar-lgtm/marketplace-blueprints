HOW THE SDR PIPELINE WORKS
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

1. Lead messages your Telegram bot
2. {{ .Values.openclaw.sdrName }} qualifies them with BANT questions
3. At end of qualifying conversation, AI outputs:

   LEAD_SUMMARY_START
   { "name": "...", "score": "hot|warm|cold", "next_action": "..." }
   LEAD_SUMMARY_END

4. Copy this JSON into Twenty CRM → People → New Person
   (v2 will auto-sync via MCP bridge)

{{- if not .Values.twenty.serverUrl }}

SERVER_URL is not set. Email notification links in Twenty CRM will use
the internal service URL and won't be clickable from outside the cluster.

To fix: get your Twenty CRM NodePort URL, then upgrade the chart:
  helm upgrade {{ .Release.Name }} b2b-sdr-agent --set twenty.serverUrl=http://NODE_IP:NODE_PORT
{{- end }}
