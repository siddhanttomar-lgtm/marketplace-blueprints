NVIDIA cuFOLIO — Quantitative Portfolio Optimization  (chart v1.0.0)
======================================================================

Deployment started. The init container is cloning cuFOLIO and running
`uv sync --extra cuda13` — this takes 5-10 minutes on first start.
Subsequent restarts reuse the cached venv and start in under 60 seconds.
